They Work Beautifully TogetherThe whole system, as one

Here's the real trick: these aren't twelve gadgets in twelve boxes. They're one operations platform, wired to a shared backbone: the work platform every app reads from and writes to (Asana today, but nothing here depends on that). Signals come in on the left, the system of record sits in the middle, and finished work goes out on the right.

Built decoupled on purpose: every machine stands on its own, so a fault in one never takes the others down.
